## Earned value rules

Planned value is calculated and established in part 1, Earned value is observed as the team finishes tasks (100/0 rule for documents meaning no credit is given until the document is fully written, sprint backlog takes story points), Actual cost is the realized expenditure calculated directly from the logged team hours multiplied by the hourly rate, and PV and AC are compared against the EV for estimation of SPI and CPI, and if either of them drop below 90% an action will be taken.
