# PMP 2: Development approach and life cycle, Author: Anas

## Life cycle

### Part 1

Due to the uncontrollable nature of part 1 and the possible new discoveries it is more convenient to use the hybrid approach, allowing the team to work on the current requirements while also accepting new input, and deliberately not the iterative approach to prevent excessive meetings, and the need for rolling-wave planning for the requirements

### Part 2

Since most requirements will have settled down, the iterative life cycle will be used, while also considering any Should or Could tasks if the schedule allows it

## Tailoring decisions

1. Part 2 will encompass 3 sprints; each being 2 weeks long
	- Reason: due to the second exams and finals, it is in our best interest to keep sprints long to prevent unnecessary meetings 

2. Events:
	- Sprint planning and retrospective : 2 hours, at the start of a sprint
	- Stand ups will be face-to-face on campus, or online if something comes up : 15 mins 3 times a week
	- Possible review session with the TA : 30 mins

3. Roles:
	- Project manager doubles as Scrum master, due to the lack of member and similarity in responsibilities, with the TA acting as an escalation point if facilitation becomes a conflict

4. Documentation:
	- All PMP are written as markdown and integrated with the repository, which gives ease of use for members and a single source of truth
