# PMP 1: Introduction and project overview, Author : Anas

- ColdChain is a responsive web app developed to help users such as quality officers and receiving pharamcists in the process of delivering vaccines, detecting excursions, creating reports and logs of the temperatures of the shipments, and preventing the use of ineffective vaccines into patients.

## Glossary

- **Excursion**: any temperature reading outside the recommended range
- **Dose**: a single administrated unit of a vaccine
- **Lot**: batch number associated with the doses
- **Logger**: a device that reads temperatures inside a shipment and logs them to the system
- **Logger calibration**: loggers must be calibrated every 12 months or they would give inaccurate results
- **Quarantine**: the receiver can send the shipments to quarantine for careful evaluation, and they can be released or disposed based on the decision of the QA
